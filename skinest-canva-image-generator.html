About Skinest

What is Skinest?

Skinest combines computer vision, the Skinest Intelligence Engine, and dermatologist expertise to give you personalised, science-backed skincare advice — from your phone.

Scan in seconds

Use your phone camera — no app needed. Our smart AI spots 15+ skin concerns in real time.

AI that understands you

Beyond the scan, our AI combines your skin type, tone classification, lifestyle and diet into one complete profile.

Dermatologist review

Every analysis is reviewed by a certified dermatologist who validates and refines the AI findings before you see results.

Why we built this

Great skin advice shouldn't be a luxury.

Most skincare advice is calibrated for Western skin. For India's diverse skin tone spectrum, humidity-driven conditions, and unique dietary factors, that advice simply doesn't work.

A good dermatologist visit costs ₹500–₹2,000 with waiting rooms, repeat appointments, and generic prescriptions. Most people end up following influencer routines that damage their skin.

We built Skinest to democratise expert skin analysis, surface products available in India (Nykaa, Minimalist, Dermaco), and make dermatologist expertise accessible to everyone — free.

😓

Generic advice fails Indian skin

FAQ

Common questions.

Is Skinest free to use?

Yes — the AI skin scan, your personalised routine, and dermatologist review are all free. We believe expert-level skin analysis shouldn't be a luxury.

How accurate is the AI analysis?

Skinest's AI detects 15+ skin conditions with high accuracy, and every result is checked by a certified dermatologist before it reaches you — combining computer vision with real medical review.

Does my photo get stored?

No. Your photo is analysed instantly and never saved. You can also delete your account at any time and everything goes with it.

What skin concerns can Skinest detect?

Acne and breakouts, dryness and dehydration, dark circles, radiance and glow, texture and pores, uneven tone, and more — 15+ attributes from a single scan.

Are products available in India?

Yes — every recommendation is a handpicked pick from brands available in India, like Nykaa, Minimalist, and Dermaco, chosen to suit your skin tone and India's weather.

How are dermatologists involved?

A real, certified dermatologist reviews every AI analysis — approving it, adding helpful notes, or flagging anything that needs extra attention — before you ever see your results.
